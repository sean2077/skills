# ai-slop-cleaner

Simplify within the authorized scope while preserving intended behavior. Prefer deletion and reuse, not new dependencies or abstractions that move complexity into callers.

## Cleanup contract

- Identify the behavior that must remain unchanged and establish a focused verification baseline. Reuse applicable observed checks or run the smallest meaningful test; when no automated seam exists, define and inspect an adequate alternative verifier.
- Remove only demonstrated duplication, dead code, needless abstraction, or masking fallback. “Generated by AI” is not evidence of a defect. Related smells may be fixed in one coherent slice; do not bundle unrelated redesign.
- Verify each meaningful behavior-risk boundary and the integrated result. Re-run checks whose evidence was invalidated, plus required project gates, rather than restarting every check after every cosmetic edit.
- Never swallow errors, weaken valid tests, or claim preserved behavior merely because the diff looks cleaner. Report what was actually checked and residual risk. A safe, sufficient alternative verifier does not need ritual no-test approval; material unverified behavior remains a blocker or an explicitly accepted limitation, not a green result.

When asked only to review, do not edit. Assess deletion safety, preserved behavior, boundary drift, and coverage; general change-set defects belong to `code-review`. A self-check is not independent approval.

Report the simplifications and observed verification to the existing delivery owner. Do not start a nested cleanup controller or require a fixed number of smell passes. Behavior changes need separate authorization.
