# prototype

Resolve a material uncertainty with a disposable experiment. Define the question, the decision it informs, and success, failure, and inconclusive signals before implementing the probe.

Bound time or attempts, cost, data, and permitted effects. Reuse project tools in an isolated workspace and inspect unfamiliar commands for side effects.

Choose an independent oracle and distinguish simulated, mocked, and real integrations. Capture enough inputs, environment, and observations to reproduce the result. Adapt the probe as evidence warrants, stopping when the decision is resolved or the agreed budget is exhausted.

Production access, publication, deployment, shared-state mutation, and retention in production code require applicable authorization.

Remove disposable artifacts you own, preserving others' work and evidence needed for the conclusion. Identify anything retained and why.

Report whether the hypothesis is supported, refuted, or inconclusive, with observations, limits, and the next decision. Keep conclusions within what the experiment actually tested.
